Shri Jaswant Hospital

We thrive on building a healthy community for
all individuals

Shri Jaswant Hospital is a 900-bedded multispecialty hospital delivering advanced, accessible, and patient-focused healthcare for over 30 years. Serving patients across Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR, and beyond, we combine experienced specialists, modern technology, and comprehensive medical services under one roof. Our facilities include advanced diagnostics, modular operation theatres, specialized ICUs, a 24x7 Emergency & Trauma Centre, Critical Care Services, an NABH-accredited Blood Centre, advanced Laboratory and Radiology Services, and a round-the-clock Pharmacy. Backed by clinical expertise, innovation, education, and research, we are committed to providing quality healthcare with compassion, ensuring better outcomes and improved patient experiences at every step.
